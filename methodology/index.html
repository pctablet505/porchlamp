Why Porchlamp

The Idea

A porch lamp left on tells a visitor they are expected. Porchlamp ranks open-source projects the same way: by what happens to a stranger's pull request, not by how many stars the project has.

The Finding

Stars do not review pull requests. In our measurements more than 1 in 8 active projects leave most pull requests from outside contributors without a human reply, review, merge or close within 14 days or since, and the more stars a project has, the more likely it is to be one of them. We call these projects black holes; they are listed under Not ranked, never ranked.

The Evidence

Every number comes from public GitHub activity: how fast a person answered outside contributors, how many of their pull requests reached a decision, whether first-time contributors came back, and how much other software depends on the project.

  • D-GATE-5

    Not a black hole

    over external PRs by non-bot authors (D-IN-1) opened in the trailing PR window and at least the minimum age, the 95% one-sided Wilson lower confidence bound (z = 1.6448536269514722) on the ignored share must not exceed the ceiling (RFC-0026: the ignored share is the share of those PRs that no maintainer reply, review, merge or close touched, within the deadline or since, when the data was read; a project that answers late is slow, not a black hole, and its 14-day unanswered share is scored by 1.4 GR_14, not gated) (a minimum population of 10 is required, else the gate is UNDECIDED rather than failed, and a repository whose PR records lack the fields the exclusions below read is UNDECIDED, neither passed nor failed: the missing fields move the share both ways); the raw share is the gr14_ghost_rate parameter, computed for such a repository without the rules those fields feed (the draft and self-close exclusions, and a maintainer close counted as a response). The gate judges only a PR record that covers the whole window. The collector records every PR opened in the window; when there are more than 2,000, it records replies and reviews on an even random sample of 1,000 of them, drawn by PR number with a fixed seed, and the gate uses the sample's share and size (RFC-0013). A record that stops short of the window's start is UNDECIDED, never judged on the newest weeks as if they were the window; the time the record starts (the cut-off) is recorded with the gate's evidence (RFC-0011). A reply or review counts when it is substantive (D-IN-2: any review, or a comment whose text, without markup, is at least 40 characters long, each Chinese, Japanese or Korean letter counting 3 times, since those scripts say in one letter about what Latin text says in three) by the repository's own people: an account that is not the PR's author, not a bot (D-IN-1), and is a member, owner or collaborator, or merged a PR in the recorded window, or closed a PR someone else opened there. Merging or closing another author's PR takes repository access, whatever association GitHub shows: org membership can be private, and committers who land changes outside GitHub show as contributors. A merge or maintainer close within the deadline counts on its own. A comment or review that follows a reply template, the first 2 words of its text once links, mentions and numbers are removed, which one account posted on at least 20 PRs of the window, on at least 80% of them within 60 seconds of the PR opening, is automation running under a person's account and does not count as a reply (RFC-0025); the same account's other replies still count. Five false-positive exclusions apply (RFC-0008, RFC-0010): draft PRs are excluded until marked ready for review (clock starts at ready_at); author self-closed PRs with no earlier maintainer response are excluded only when the author closed them within the 14-day response deadline from clock start, and a later self-close with no response counts as unanswered; accounts under 90 days old (MIN_ACCOUNT_AGE_DAYS) are excluded; maintainer closes within 14 days count as responses; and the 14-day observation age is judged from clock start against min(now, observed_at)

    Reasons
    black_hole
    Cutoffs
    min_n10min_age_days14response_deadline_days14max_zero_response_share0.5

    PR workflow

    >= 1 external PR (bots included: the gate asks whether the repository works through GitHub pull requests at all) decided (merged or closed) in the trailing decision window, read from the recorded PR window, the 365-day author history and the 30 newest PRs, or off-platform markers present (routed, not failed); neither means unrated: no_external_workflow, but only when those records hold the repository's whole PR history (the 30 newest were fewer than 30, and no window or history read beside them is truncated): otherwise the gate is UNDECIDED, because a PR opened before the records may have been decided inside the window. Each record shows decisions up to its own fetch. 8

What a missing input means

third_party_gap
A third party (a registry, OpenSSF, deps.dev) never provided the fact; no maintainer action can close this gap.
collector_failure
The collector attempted to measure this input and failed (a fetch error, a stale window anchor); a retry can still measure it.
insufficient_n
The input was measured but its sample size fell below the parameter's minimum n; the measurement is recorded but scored as unmeasured.
not_applicable
The parameter is undefined for this repository's class by a closed, design-level rule (D-MISS-5); excluded from both the numerator and the denominator of coverage.
no_population
The parameter is ECDF-normalised and no rated, measured repository exists in its ecosystem or globally at this snapshot to build a population from.

Limits

  • Not a verdict on the people who maintain these projects. Review time is scarce, and popular projects receive far more pull requests than anyone can read.
  • GitHub only. Projects that review on Gerrit or a mailing list are left undecided, not failed.
  • One six-month window. The numbers will change at the next snapshot.
